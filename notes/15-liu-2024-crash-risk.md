## Research question

Does greenwashing in ESG reports raise the risk of a **stock price crash** (a sudden large fall in
a share price)?

## Method

† Chinese listed firms, 2014–2021, with greenwashing measured as **ESG disclosure minus ESG
performance** following Yu et al. (note 14). The abstract confirms the regression design looks at
the effect of "ESG report greenwashing" on crash risk, tests the role of earnings management,
corporate governance and voluntary disclosure, and examines an information-asymmetry mechanism.

## Headline results

From the abstract:

- **ESG report greenwashing increases stock price crash risk.**
- In companies with **high earnings management**, the link is positive, and greenwashing and
  earnings management are **complementary**.
- **Better internal and external governance** can suppress the effect.
- The effect is **larger** in the **voluntary disclosure** and **private enterprise** samples.
- The mechanism: greenwashing **intensifies information asymmetry**, which raises crash risk.

## Reality anchor

**Ratings gap** † (disclosure score minus performance score). The brief's claim that this is the
Yu et al. measure is not confirmed in the abstract.

## How it relates to our project

It shows that a greenwashing measure can be **validated against a future market outcome**. That
gives us a tie-breaker idea: if our indices disagree, the one that best predicts a later outcome
has the stronger claim to measure something real. This is **predictive validity** (hypothesis H6;
Improvement 9). A caution that the same idea carries: market outcomes can themselves reflect
visibility, so conduct outcomes (such as later EPA penalties) should take priority.

## Limitations

- Based on the abstract only.
- The finding is a statistical link between a ratings-gap measure and crash risk; it does not
  show that the underlying firms were actually misleading anyone.
- The voluntary-disclosure result is interesting but runs against the simple idea that mandatory
  rules always help; this is a case where the regime changes the result.
