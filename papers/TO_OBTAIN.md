# Papers to obtain

Fifteen of the 19 additional papers are not in `papers/`. Four are (see `SOURCES.md`).
Nothing here was bypassed or taken from a shadow library: these are the papers we could not
legally download automatically. Each link below goes to the publisher through the DOI, which
should route through your institute's library access if you are signed in or on campus.

Until a PDF is added, the matching note in `notes/` is based on the abstract and Crossref
metadata only, and says so.

Notes on a few of these:

- **P13, P14, P18, P21:** the research brief said accepted manuscripts exist in repositories
  (CORE/Exeter ORE, Birkbeck BIROn, Augsburg OPUS). We could not find them through OpenAlex. If you
  can locate one, it is a legal free copy. Check the repository's licence statement before adding it.
- **P10, P21, P24:** earlier working-paper versions are free on SSRN/ECGI, but they are not the
  published article, so numbers may differ. Use them only as a stopgap and cite the published version.
- **P9:** a Figshare record exists but is marked *In Copyright* with no file, so it is not open.
