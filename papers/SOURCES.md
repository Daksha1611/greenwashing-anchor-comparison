## 1. Davidescu et al. (2026) — *verified, no changes*

Davidescu, A. A., Manta, E. M., Bîrlan, I., Miler, A. M., & Niță, A. S. C. (2026). Detecting
greenwashing in ESG disclosure: An NLP-based analysis of Central and Eastern European firms.
*Sustainability, 18*(3), 1486. https://doi.org/10.3390/su18031486

- **File:** `sustainability-18-01486-v2.pdf`
- **Publisher:** MDPI, Basel · Open access (CC BY)
- **Dates:** received 20 Dec 2025 · accepted 22 Jan 2026 · published 2 Feb 2026
- **Note:** `notes/01-davidescu-2026-gsi.md`, `synthesis/gsi-formula.md`

### Indexing status — read this before citing

*Sustainability* is **peer-reviewed and indexed in Scopus, SCIE and SSCI**. However:

- **Norway** removed *Sustainability* from its approved publication list (the Norwegian Register
  for Scientific Journals / NSD) in **2022**, downgrading it to level 0 — i.e. it no longer counts
  toward publication credit in the Norwegian system.
- **Finland's** JUFO (Publication Forum) rates it **Level 0**.

MDPI's rapid-review model attracts recurring criticism over review depth. This does not
invalidate the paper, and it remains legitimately citable and indexed — but this project relies on
it heavily, and the **specific problems documented in `synthesis/gsi-formula.md` §3 (a formula
that omits the quantity it claims to measure, two incompatible statements of the sentiment scale,
and statistics that contradict the paper's own tables) are consistent with limited review
scrutiny.** Cite it accurately, and do not treat its GSI as a validated instrument.
